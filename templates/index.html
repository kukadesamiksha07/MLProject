Architecture

How JobShield AI Detects Scams

A transparent end-to-end Machine Learning pipeline transforming unstructured job descriptions into calibrated risk probabilities.

01

Text Ingestion

Raw employment postings, including job titles, descriptions, and compensation terms, are collected and merged into an unstructured text stream.

02

Text Preprocessing

The text is normalized to lowercase, punctuation is handled, and common English stop words are removed to highlight discriminative phrasing.

03

TF-IDF Vectorization

50,000 unigrams and bigrams are extracted with sublinear term-frequency scaling, penalizing common terms while emphasizing unique fraud signals.

04

Logistic Regression

Class-weighted Logistic Regression evaluates feature weights and computes the log-odds of fraud, passing through a sigmoid activation curve.

05

Calibrated Scoring

Outputs a clear verdict, an exact fraud probability percentage, and pinpoints top mathematical linguistic signals for transparent decision explainability.

Algorithm Fundamentals

About the Machine Learning Engine

Understanding the math and natural language processing techniques powering JobShield AI.

Feature Extraction

TF-IDF Vectorization

Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) converts job descriptions into numerical vectors based on how informative each word or phrase is.

Common words like "the", "and", or "job" appear in virtually every posting and receive low weights. In contrast, distinctive n-grams such as "cashier check", "wire transfer", or "registration fee" appear rarely across legitimate jobs and receive high importance values.

# TF-IDF Pipeline Configuration
TfidfVectorizer(
  stop_words="english",
  max_features=50000,
  ngram_range=(1, 2), # Unigrams & Bigrams
  sublinear_tf=True
)
Classification

Logistic Regression

Logistic Regression is a statistical classification algorithm that computes a linear combination of all TF-IDF feature weights and maps the result through a sigmoid activation function to output a probability between 0% and 100%.

In high-dimensional text classification (50,000 features), Logistic Regression is exceptionally well-suited: it avoids deep neural network overfitting, provides sub-second inference, and produces mathematically interpretable coefficients.

# Sigmoid Mathematical Function
P(Fraud | X) = 1 / (1 + e^-(w · X + b))

# Classifier Configuration
LogisticRegression(
  class_weight="balanced",
  max_iter=1000,
  random_state=42
)
Safety & Compliance

Job Scam Red Flags & Disclaimers

Essential safety rules every job seeker must follow to avoid recruitment fraud.

Critical Recruitment Red Flags

  • Upfront Fees: Legitimate employers will never ask you to pay for training, onboarding, software licenses, or background checks.
  • Overpayment / Cashier Checks: Scammers send fake checks to "buy home equipment" and instruct you to wire excess funds back.
  • Chat-Only Interviews: Real companies do not conduct formal hiring exclusively over Telegram, WhatsApp, or Google Chat text.
  • Generic Email Domains: Watch out for recruiters using free webmail (@gmail.com, @yahoo.com) rather than corporate domains.
  • Unrealistic Pay for Low Effort: Promises of $50–$100/hr for simple data entry with no prior experience or interview are nearly always fraudulent.

System Limitations & Scope

JobShield AI evaluates text using learned statistical patterns from historical data. It does not perform active background checks, corporate registry lookups, or criminal investigations.

Sophisticated scammers occasionally copy authentic job descriptions verbatim from legitimate companies. In such cases, text analysis alone may classify the description as legitimate. Always verify:

Check official career portals directly
Confirm recruiter identity on LinkedIn
Never share banking PINs, SSNs, or crypto
Use JobShield AI as an initial screening tool, not a legal guarantee.
